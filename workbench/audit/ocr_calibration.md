# OCR reconciliation — Phase 1 calibration

Generated by `scripts/ocr_calibrate.py`. Scope: 2015 (GS1-3) and 2022 (GS1-4) — the years whose text layer already produced verdicts. The nine scan-only years are untouched. OCR: 300 DPI, tesseract eng only; the `[\x20-\x7E]+` filter strips Devanagari noise. The text-layer verdict is treated as ground truth. Every OCR verdict is `ocr_derived=true` and is never written into or treated as equivalent to the text-layer CSV.

Two scorings are reported per question:
- **chunk-port** — reconcile_gs_sources' chunk-based `verdict_for` at its 95/80/90 thresholds, run unchanged on OCR text (the literal 'same matcher').
- **full-text** — `partial_ratio` of the question against the WHOLE OCR paper, bypassing the chunker.

These flips are a HARNESS artifact, not an OCR-quality signal. OCR inserts spurious numeric line-starts (marks like `150`, stray digits), so `chunk_raw` splits a single question across chunk boundaries. Worked examples:
- 2022 GS2 q21: present in the OCR at `partial_ratio`=**100** against the whole paper, but the chunk holding it is fragmented so the per-chunk best falls to 50.5 -> false `orphan`.
- 2015 GS2 q4: genuinely absent (**49.5** vs whole paper) yet a tiny OCR fragment chunk that is a substring of the question inflates `partial_ratio` to 100 -> false `exact`.

Conclusion: the chunk-based matcher does NOT transfer to OCR text. Score OCR against the full paper. (The same fragmentation also produced at least one false orphan in the text-layer run — see 2015 GS3 q1 below.)

## Threshold recommendation (full-text, derived from THIS data)

- authentic full-text score: min **92.0**, and all 105 authentic rows sit at **>= 92.0** (median 100).
- orphan full-text score: **73** of 74 sit at **<= 74.3**; **1** scores at 100 — a text-layer false-orphan that OCR recovers (see below), not a genuine orphan.
- Clean band separating the genuine populations: **(74.3, 92.0)** — a ~18-point gap.

**Recommendation (phase-1, this corpus): OCR match cut = 85** (>= 85 = match, 75-85 = review, < 75 = no-match/orphan). At 85 every genuine authentic row (min 92.0) is a match and every genuine orphan (max 74.3) is not; the 75-85 review band is empty here but kept as a safety margin for other papers.

Do NOT carry over 95/80: OCR true-matches spread down to 92 (a 95 cut alone would false-reject the 92-95 authentic rows), and — more importantly — the chunk pipeline that 95/80 assumes must be replaced by full-text scoring.

### Does 85 separate long-form as cleanly as short-form?

- **long-form**: authentic n=6 min **95.2**; orphan n=6 max **47.9** -> gap **47** pts.
- **short-form**: authentic n=96 min **92.0**; orphan n=73 max **74.3** -> gap **18** pts.
- long-form authentic is **NOT depressed** vs short-form (min 95.2 vs 92.0).

**Yes — 85 separates long-form at least as cleanly as short-form.** Every long-form authentic (95.2) is >= 85 and every long-form orphan (47.9) is < 85. The long-form gap (47 pts) is wider than the short-form gap (18 pts) — a single **85** cut holds for both lengths, and the earlier long-form gap is now closed with a real orphan sample (n=6).
