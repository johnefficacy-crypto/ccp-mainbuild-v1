## ACTO-001 · L1 · easy · FEMA — residential status under s.2(v) ⚠

Under s.2(v) of FEMA, the basic day-count test that makes an individual a "person resident in India" for FY 2026-27 is that he resided in India for:

- **A.** 182 days or more during FY 2026-27  _(error: income-tax style current-year test imported)_
- **B.** More than 182 days during FY 2026-27  _(error: current FY used instead of the preceding FY)_
- **C.** Not less than 120 days during FY 2025-26  _(error: LLP designated-partner residency test (120 days) imported)_
- **D.** More than 182 days during FY 2025-26 ✅

**Working**

1. s.2(v)(i): a person residing in India for more than 182 days during the course of the preceding financial year.
2. The test looks back to the preceding FY (FY 2025-26 for FY 2026-27), and 182 days exactly is not enough.
3. Even if the count is met, the purpose-based exclusions in s.2(v)(i)(A)/(B) can override it.

**Formula:** Resident (individual) = > 182 days in preceding FY, subject to purpose exclusions  
**Trap:** Two traps: 'preceding' FY, and 'more than' 182 days.

## ACTO-002 · L1 · easy · FEMA — civil character and contrast with FERA ⚠

A contravention of a provision of FEMA (other than the special regime for assets held abroad) is, by its basic design:

- **A.** A civil wrong visited with a monetary penalty imposed on adjudication ✅
- **B.** A criminal offence punishable with imprisonment and fine on prosecution  _(error: FERA, 1973 approach)_
- **C.** An offence with a statutory presumption of culpable mental state  _(error: FERA s.59 presumption carried over)_
- **D.** A cognizable, non-bailable offence investigated by the police  _(error: confused with PMLA-type offences)_

**Working**

1. FEMA replaced FERA from 1 June 2000 and converted foreign-exchange violations into civil contraventions.
2. s.13 provides monetary penalty after adjudication under s.16; imprisonment arises only as civil imprisonment for non-payment of penalty (s.14).
3. FERA treated violations as criminal offences and presumed culpable mental state.

**Formula:** FEMA: contravention → adjudication → penalty (civil)  
**Trap:** Civil imprisonment under s.14 is a recovery tool, not punishment for the contravention.
