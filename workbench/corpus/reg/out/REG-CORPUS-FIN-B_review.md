## FINB-001 · L1 · easy · ICDR Regulations — essentials ⚠

Under Regulation 6(1) of the SEBI (ICDR) Regulations, 2018, which of the following are among the eligibility conditions for a main-board IPO?

1. Net tangible assets of at least ₹3 crore in each of the preceding three full years, of which not more than 50% is held in monetary assets
2. An average operating profit of at least ₹15 crore during the preceding three years, with operating profit in each of those years
3. A net worth of at least ₹5 crore in each of the preceding three full years

Select the correct answer.

- **A.** 2 only  _(error: wrongly rejects statement 1: NTA test is ₹3 crore each year with the 50% monetary-asset cap)_
- **B.** 1 and 2 only ✅
- **C.** 1 only  _(error: wrongly rejects statement 2: operating-profit test is ₹15 crore average with profit in each year)_
- **D.** All of 1, 2 and 3  _(error: wrongly accepts statement 3: the net-worth test is ₹1 crore in each of three years, not ₹5 crore)_

**Working**

1. Reg 6(1)(a): NTA ≥ ₹3 crore in each of the preceding 3 full years (restated, consolidated), not more than 50% in monetary assets (cap relaxed if the IPO is entirely an offer for sale).
2. Reg 6(1)(b): average operating profit ≥ ₹15 crore over the preceding 3 years, with operating profit in each year.
3. Reg 6(1)(c): net worth ≥ ₹1 crore in each of the preceding 3 years — so statement 3 overstates it.

**Formula:** Reg 6(1): NTA ₹3 cr / Op. profit avg ₹15 cr / Net worth ₹1 cr  
**Trap:** The ₹1 crore net-worth floor is often inflated to ₹5 crore or confused with the ₹3 crore NTA figure.
