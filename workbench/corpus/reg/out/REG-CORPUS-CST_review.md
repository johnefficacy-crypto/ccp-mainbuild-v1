## CST-001 · L1 · easy · 5S and the sixth S

In the 5S workplace-organisation system, the step called **Seiketsu** refers to:

- **A.** Sort — removing items that are not needed at the workstation  _(error: confuses Seiketsu with Seiri)_

- **C.** Sustain — building self-discipline to keep the system alive  _(error: confuses Seiketsu with Shitsuke)_

**Working**

**Formula:** 5S sequence: Sort → Set in order → Shine → Standardise → Sustain (+ Safety)  
**Trap:** Standardise (4th S) and Sustain (5th S) are the pair most often swapped.

## CST-002 · L1 · easy · Cellular manufacturing / one-piece flow

Which of the following best describes **cellular manufacturing**?

- **A.** Grouping similar machines into separate departments such as a lathe shop and a drilling shop  _(error: describes functional (process) layout)_

- **D.** Producing in large batches so that set-up costs are spread over more units  _(error: batch-and-queue logic that cells are meant to replace)_

**Working**

**Formula:** Cell = product-family layout + one-piece flow  
**Trap:** A functional layout groups SIMILAR machines; a cell groups DIFFERENT machines.

**Trap:** 'Every company' is the give-away — cost records apply only to notified industries above thresholds.
