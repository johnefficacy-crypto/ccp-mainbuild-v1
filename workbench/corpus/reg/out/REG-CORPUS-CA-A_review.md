## CAA-001 · L1 · easy · Adjournment when quorum is absent ⚠

In a public company, if a quorum is not present within half an hour of the time appointed for a general meeting (not called on requisition of members), the meeting:

- **A.** Stands dissolved and must be called afresh by a new notice of 21 clear days to members  _(error: requisition-meeting cancellation rule applied to all meetings)_
- **B.** Stands adjourned to the same day next week, same time and place, or as the Board fixes ✅
- **C.** Proceeds at once, the members present being deemed to constitute the quorum for it  _(error: adjourned-meeting rule applied to the original meeting)_
- **D.** Stands adjourned to the same day in the next month, same time and place, or as fixed  _(error: period of adjournment misstated)_

**Working**

1. s.103(2)(a): meeting stands adjourned to the same day next week, same time and place, or other date/time/place determined by the Board.
2. Only at the adjourned meeting do the members present constitute the quorum (s.103(3)).

**Formula:** s.103(2): no quorum in 30 minutes → adjourned one week (or as Board fixes)  
**Trap:** 'Members present are the quorum' applies only at the adjourned meeting.

(ii) a loan to Vistar Pvt Ltd, of which Rohan is a member, for Vistar's principal business;
(iii) a housing loan to a Whole-time Director under a scheme approved by members by special resolution.

Which is prohibited irrespective of any shareholder approval?

- **A.** (i) only ✅
- **B.** (i) and (ii) only  _(error: loan to a private company in which a director is a member treated as absolutely prohibited (pre-2018 view))_
- **C.** (ii) only  _(error: firm in which a director's relative is partner treated as permissible)_
- **D.** (i), (ii) and (iii)  _(error: service-scheme exception for MD/WTD overlooked)_

**Working**

1. s.185(1)(b): no loan to a firm in which a director or his relative is a partner → (i) prohibited.
2. s.185(2): loan to 'any person in whom the director is interested' (incl. a private company of which he is a member) allowed with special resolution and use for principal business → (ii) permissible.
3. s.185(3)(a): loan to MD/WTD under a scheme approved by members by special resolution → (iii) permissible.

**Formula:** s.185(1) absolute bar (director, director of holding, partner, firm with director/relative as partner); s.185(2) SR route  
**Trap:** The relative-partner firm is caught by the absolute bar.

## CAA-130 · L4 · hard · Appointment, DIN and intimation to the Registrar ⚠

**Case — Sundaram Logistics Ltd.** Sundaram, a public company that is neither a holding nor a subsidiary company, plans to appoint **Rohan Mehta** (who already holds a DIN) as an additional director on 10 November 2026. Rohan is currently a director of 7 public companies, 2 private companies that are subsidiaries of public companies, 6 other private companies and 4 dormant companies, and an alternate director in 1 other private company (not a subsidiary of a public company). An existing Sundaram director, **Leena Rao**, is also a director of Kanak Traders Pvt Ltd, which has not filed its financial statements or annual returns for FY 2022-23, 2023-24 and 2024-25.

Which statement about the filings for Rohan's appointment is correct?

- **A.** Consent and return both to be filed within 60 days of appointment (by 9 January 2027)  _(error: filing window doubled)_
- **B.** Consent to be filed within 30 days (by 10 December 2026); company's return also within 30 days ✅
- **C.** Sundaram must obtain a fresh DIN for Rohan specific to this company before he can act  _(error: s.155 one-DIN rule ignored)_
- **D.** No filing is needed, since an additional director holds office only till the next AGM  _(error: additional director treated as outside s.152(5)/s.170(2))_

**Working**

1. s.152(5): a person appointed shall not act unless his consent is filed with the Registrar within 30 days of appointment.
2. s.170(2): return containing particulars of appointment filed within 30 days.
3. s.155: an individual holding a DIN shall not obtain another.

**Formula:** Consent (DIR-2) and return (DIR-12) — 30 days each; one DIN for life  
**Trap:** An additional director is a director for every filing purpose.
