## CAB-001 · L1 · easy · s.123 sources of dividend ⚠

Under section 123 of the Companies Act, 2013, which of the following is NOT a permissible source for declaring dividend?

- **A.** Balance in the securities premium account ✅
- **B.** Profits of the current financial year arrived at after providing for depreciation under Schedule II  _(error: permitted source under s.123(1)(a))_
- **C.** Undistributed profits of earlier years arrived at after providing for depreciation  _(error: permitted source under s.123(1)(b))_
- **D.** Money provided by the Central or a State Government for payment of dividend in pursuance of a guarantee  _(error: permitted source under s.123(1)(c))_

**Working**

1. s.123(1) permits dividend only out of (a) current-year profits after depreciation, (b) undistributed past profits after depreciation, (c) both, or government money under a guarantee.
2. Securities premium can be applied only for the purposes listed in s.52(2) — distribution as dividend is not one of them.

**Formula:** s.123(1) sources; s.52(2) applications of securities premium  
**Trap:** Securities premium is a capital receipt; it can fund bonus shares but never a cash dividend.

## CAB-002 · L3 · hard · s.123 sources of dividend ⚠

| Depreciation of an earlier year not provided in that year | 0.30 |

- **C.** ₹7.05 crore  _(error: brought-forward loss and arrear depreciation not set off)_

**Trap:** All three adjustments are mandatory; transfer to reserves is optional and does not reduce the ceiling.

## CAB-003 · L3 · hard · s.123 sources of dividend ⚠

Hemadri Mills Ltd has incurred a loss of ₹1.50 crore in FY 2026-27 and proposes to declare an equity dividend of 14% out of accumulated profits (free reserves). Latest audited figures: paid-up equity capital ₹40.00 crore, free reserves ₹25.00 crore. Dividend rates for the three immediately preceding years were 12%, 15% and 9%. Under Rule 3 of the Companies (Declaration and Payment of Dividend) Rules, 2014, the maximum equity dividend that can be declared is:

- **A.** ₹5.60 crore  _(error: proposed 14% rate not capped at the three-year average)_
- **B.** ₹5.00 crore  _(error: only the one-tenth withdrawal cap applied; average-rate cap ignored)_
- **C.** ₹6.50 crore  _(error: one-tenth cap used without first setting off the current-year loss)_
- **D.** ₹4.80 crore ✅

**Working**

1. Average of preceding three years' rates = 12% → dividend cap ₹4.80 crore.
2. Withdrawal cap = 1/10 × (₹40.00 crore + ₹25.00 crore) = ₹6.50 crore; first ₹1.50 crore must absorb the current loss → ₹5.00 crore available.
3. Reserves after withdrawal must stay ≥ 15% of paid-up = ₹6.00 crore; not binding here.
4. Maximum = lowest of the caps = ₹4.80 crore.

**Formula:** Rule 3: rate ≤ 3-yr average; draw ≤ 1/10 (PUC + FR); loss set off first; residual reserves ≥ 15% PUC  
**Trap:** The average-rate cap is on the rate; the one-tenth cap is on the total withdrawal including loss set-off.

## CAB-004 · L3 · hard · s.123 sources of dividend ⚠

Applying all conditions of Rule 3 of the Companies (Declaration and Payment of Dividend) Rules, 2014, the maximum equity dividend it can declare out of free reserves is:

- **A.** ₹4.00 crore  _(error: 15%-of-paid-up residual reserve floor ignored)_
- **B.** ₹5.00 crore  _(error: only the average-rate cap applied)_
- **C.** ₹0.50 crore ✅
