## ECO-001 · L1 · easy · GDP, GNP, NNP and NDP concepts

The difference between Gross National Product and Gross Domestic Product of an economy (both at market prices) is equal to:

- **A.** Net current transfers from abroad  _(error: treats remittances/transfers as factor income)_
- **B.** Consumption of fixed capital  _(error: confuses gross vs net gap)_
- **C.** Net factor income from abroad ✅
- **D.** Net indirect taxes  _(error: confuses market-price vs factor-cost gap with domestic vs national gap)_

**Working**

1. Domestic → national: add factor incomes earned by residents abroad and deduct factor incomes earned by non-residents here.
2. GNP_MP − GDP_MP = Net factor income from abroad (NFIA).
3. Net indirect taxes separate MP from FC; depreciation separates gross from net.

**Formula:** GNP = GDP + NFIA  
**Trap:** Three different 'bridges' — NFIA, depreciation, net indirect taxes — each links a different pair of aggregates.
