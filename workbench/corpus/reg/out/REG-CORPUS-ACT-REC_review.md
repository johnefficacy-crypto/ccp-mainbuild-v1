## ACTR-001 · L1 · easy · IBC — CIRP initiation, default threshold and applicants (s.4, 7, 9, 10) ⚠

Under section 4 of the Insolvency and Bankruptcy Code, 2016, read with the Central Government notification of March 2020, the minimum amount of default for initiating the corporate insolvency resolution process (CIRP) against a corporate debtor is:

- **A.** ₹1 lakh  _(error: original s.4 figure, superseded by the 2020 notification)_

1. s.4 fixes the minimum default at ₹1 lakh but lets the Central Government raise it up to ₹1 crore.

**Trap:** ₹10 lakh is the separate threshold for PPIRP (Chapter III-A), not for ordinary CIRP.

## ACTR-002 · L2 · medium · IBC — CIRP initiation, default threshold and applicants (s.4, 7, 9, 10) ⚠

Kestrel Bank is a financial creditor of Veltrix Components Ltd. Position on the date of its application under section 7:

| Item | ₹ lakh |
|---|---:|
| Term-loan instalments due and unpaid | 42.00 |
| Interest due and unpaid on the term loan | 21.50 |
| Letter of credit devolved and not reimbursed on demand | 38.00 |
| Term-loan instalments falling due over the next 18 months | 60.00 |

Taking the notified minimum default, the amount of default and the maintainability of the application are:

- **A.** ₹63.50 lakh — the application is not maintainable  _(error: devolved LC ignored; only the term loan counted)_
- **B.** ₹161.50 lakh — the application is maintainable  _(error: instalments not yet due counted as default)_
- **C.** ₹101.50 lakh — the application is maintainable ✅
- **D.** ₹80.00 lakh — the application is not maintainable  _(error: unpaid interest excluded from default)_

**Working**

1. Default = debt that has become due and payable and is not paid (s.3(12)).
2. Due and unpaid: 42.0 + 21.5 + 38.0 = ₹101.50 lakh; future instalments are not yet due.
3. ₹101.50 lakh ≥ ₹100 lakh (₹1 crore), so the s.7 application is maintainable.

**Formula:** Default (s.3(12)) = amount due and unpaid; compare with ₹1 crore (s.4)  
**Trap:** Interest that is due is part of the financial debt in default; unmatured instalments are not.

## ACTR-008 · L3 · hard · IBC — moratorium, IRP/RP and CoC decision-making ⚠

**Assertion (A):** At its first meeting, the committee of creditors may replace the interim resolution professional with another resolution professional by a vote of 51% of the voting share.

**Reason (R):** Under section 21(8), all decisions of the committee of creditors are taken by a vote of not less than 51% of the voting share of the financial creditors, unless otherwise provided in the Code.

- **A.** Both A and R are true, but R is not the correct explanation of A  _(error: Assertion treated as true)_
- **B.** A is true, but R is false  _(error: s.21(8) default rule wrongly rejected)_
- **C.** Both A and R are true, and R is the correct explanation of A  _(error: s.22(2) special majority of 66% overlooked)_

**Working**

1. s.21(8): default threshold 51% 'save as otherwise provided'.
2. s.22(2): the first CoC meeting may resolve to appoint the IRP as RP or replace him — by a vote of not less than 66%.
3. So A is false (needs 66%), R is a true statement of the default rule.

**Formula:** s.22(2): 66% to replace IRP; s.21(8): 51% default  
**Trap:** The 51% rule is displaced wherever the Code prescribes 66%.
