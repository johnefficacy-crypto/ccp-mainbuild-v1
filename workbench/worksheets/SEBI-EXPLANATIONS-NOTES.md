### 1.1 DISPUTED — the keyed answer is, in my judgement, wrong

#### 2020 Q8 · economics · `cd6c36b0-bb36-4b57-a583-b7918c915425`

**Stem.** Which of the following will not affect Current Account Deficit?

- **Keyed option:** (e) Natural Resources
- **Proposed option:** (a) Capital Inflow
- **Reason.** Capital inflow belongs to the capital and financial account, not the current account. It finances a current account deficit; it does not enter the computation of one. Natural resources, by contrast, drive import dependence and export capacity and therefore do affect the CAD. The key should be (a) Capital Inflow.

#### 2024 Q10 · companies-act · `cab8bf24-a2dc-47cb-a1ef-7152fa49fa56`

**Stem.** Transfer of Securities related to equity, debt and other securities must be delivered within ___ maximum days of execution of the company.

- **Keyed option:** (c) 60 days
- **Proposed option:** (a) 30 days
- **Reason.** Section 56(4)(c) of the Companies Act, 2013 requires delivery of certificates within one month from the date of receipt by the company of the instrument of transfer or intimation of transmission. That is thirty days, option (a). Sixty days corresponds to the two-month periods in section 56(4)(a) and (b), which cover subscribers to the memorandum and allotment respectively, not transfer. If the item intends allotment rather than transfer, the stem needs rewriting; as written, it asks about transfer.

### 1.2 AMBIGUOUS — more than one option is defensible, or the data is inconsistent

#### 2020 Q3 · economics · `a4bb3bb7-3374-4f76-9a75-6fbb917b77b8`

**Stem.** From the following, select the correct phase of business cycle.

- **Keyed option:** (a) Expansion, Recession, Trough, Recovery
- **Proposed option:** none — the item needs rewriting, not re-keying. The competing options are named in the reason.
- **Reason.** The cycle is circular, so any rotation of Expansion - Recession - Trough - Recovery is an equally valid answer. Option (a) Expansion, Recession, Trough, Recovery; option (b) Recession, Trough, Recovery, Expansion; and option (d) Recovery, Expansion, Recession, Trough are all rotations of the same sequence and all internally correct. Only (c) and (e) are genuinely wrong. Without a stated starting point the question has three defensible answers. The item needs a fixed anchor (e.g. 'starting from the peak') to be single-keyed.

#### 2022 Q1 · costing · `8408867a-f42c-4b07-b7ab-0b60f313af95`

**Stem.** Which of the following is a modern management accounting technique?

- **Keyed option:** (c) Responsibility Accounting
- **Proposed option:** none — the item needs rewriting, not re-keying. The competing options are named in the reason.
- **Reason.** Human Resource Accounting (option a) is also routinely listed as a modern management accounting technique in standard texts, alongside responsibility accounting. Both (a) and (c) are defensible and the stem gives no criterion to choose between them. The item needs 'which of the following is NOT' framing or a single modern technique among clearly traditional ones.

#### 2024 Q2 · economics · `63945515-c67a-4cc7-96b2-636786fe109b`

**Stem.** Which of the following shows the relationship between price and quantity demanded?

- **Keyed option:** (e) Elasticity
- **Proposed option:** none — the item needs rewriting, not re-keying. The competing options are named in the reason.
- **Reason.** Option (c) Demand is at least as defensible as the key. The law of demand and the demand curve are precisely what 'shows the relationship between price and quantity demanded'; elasticity measures the DEGREE of that responsiveness. The stem needs to say 'measures the responsiveness of' rather than 'shows the relationship between' for the keyed answer to be uniquely correct.

#### 2024 Q17 · companies-act · `1efc0ee0-a75d-4244-aba9-06cfc7eb6680`

**Stem.** Public offers of securities must be in _______________.

- **Keyed option:** (b) Demat Form
- **Proposed option:** none — the item needs rewriting, not re-keying. The competing options are named in the reason.
- **Reason.** Option (e) 'Electronic Form' is a synonym of the keyed 'Demat Form' — dematerialised holding IS electronic holding — so two options are correct as written. Equally, options (a), (c) and (d) are three synonyms of the same wrong answer. The item needs one wrong-answer cluster reduced and the synonym (e) removed before it can be single-keyed.

#### 2025 Q6 · economics · `05c23b96-e594-43ee-8e1e-ad9d4342734a`

**Stem.** Calculate National Income and Personal Income from the following data: 1. NNP at Market Price Rs 1000 2. Subsidy Rs 50 3. Direct Tax Rs 80 4. Corporate Tax Rs 70 5. Indirect Tax Rs 120 6. Miscellaneous Government Receipts Rs 40 7. Undistributed Profits Rs 60 8. Corporate Tax Rs 80

- **Keyed option:** (b) National Income Rs 930; Personal Income Rs 790
- **Proposed option:** none — the item needs rewriting, not re-keying. The competing options are named in the reason.
- **Reason.** The data set is internally inconsistent: 'Corporate Tax' is listed twice with different values (item 4 = Rs 70 and item 8 = Rs 80), and 'Miscellaneous Government Receipts Rs 40' is never used. The keyed personal income of 790 is reachable only by using the Rs 80 figure, ignoring the Rs 70 figure, and ignoring the miscellaneous receipts. Using Rs 70 instead gives Rs 800, which is not an option. The National Income leg (930) is unambiguous and by itself selects the keyed option, so the item is answerable, but the personal-income data must be corrected.

#### 2025 Q21 · finance · `6be4c398-bdab-4a7e-9b96-a20ce6a60297`

**Stem.** What is the minimum investment amount required for subscribing to Non-Convertible Preference Shares (NCPs)?

- **Keyed option:** (e) ₹1,00,000
- **Proposed option:** none — the item needs rewriting, not re-keying. The competing options are named in the reason.
- **Reason.** This corpus keys BOTH answers for the same instruments. The 2024 item on the minimum face value of NCDs and NCRPS is keyed Rs 10,000 (the post-amendment figure), while this 2025 item is keyed Rs 1,00,000 (the pre-amendment figure). SEBI reduced the minimum face value for privately placed non-convertible securities from Rs 1 lakh to Rs 10,000 subject to conditions, so Rs 10,000 is the current position and option (a) is defensible here. The two items must be reconciled to one regulatory vintage before either is published.

### 6.3 Constraints the apply step must respect

- `unique (question_id, explanation_source_type)` — at most one `platform_original` explanation per question, so
  the apply step must be an upsert on that pair, not a blind insert.
- **Fail-closed verification** (`pyq_question_explanations_guard`, BEFORE INSERT OR UPDATE):
  `reviewer_status = 'verified'` is rejected unless the licence is cleared, `ambiguity_status = 'none'`,
  `final_answer_option_id` is not null, and `reviewed_by` / `reviewed_at` are both set. These rows are inserted
  `pending`, so the gate is not engaged — but note that the 8 DISPUTED/AMBIGUOUS rows can **never** be verified
  while their `ambiguity_status` is non-`none`. Resolving the key is a prerequisite to verification, by design.
- **Same-question answer integrity** — both option-reference columns are checked against `pyq_options.question_id`
  by the same trigger, so a mis-mapped option id fails loudly rather than silently.
- **Content/trust-edit downgrade** — editing any learner-facing or provenance field on an already-verified row
  forces it back to `needs_correction` and clears reviewer identity. Re-running the apply step over verified rows
  would therefore silently un-verify them; the apply step should skip rows not in `pending`.
- **RLS and grants** — admin/service-role only (`pyq_question_explanations_admin_all`, `is_admin(auth.uid())`);
  explicit table grants to `service_role` and `authenticated`. `anon` has nothing. Unverified rows cannot reach
  learners.
