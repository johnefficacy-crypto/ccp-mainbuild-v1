*An analysis of every Political Science & International Relations optional question we hold, 1991–2026.*

### How this was measured

We hold every UPSC optional question our sources record from 1991 to 2026 —
1,299 questions for this subject — each tagged to a specific line of the official
syllabus. For every syllabus topic we then count the **distinct years it was
asked in**, how evenly those years are spaced, and how recently it last
appeared.

Counting distinct years matters more than counting questions: a topic asked
twice in one paper and never again is not a pattern, while one asked once every
third year for two decades is.

Two honest limits. The corpus is compiled from published question collections,
not from UPSC's own archive, which only publishes papers from 2016 onward — so
a small number of questions may be mis-transcribed at source. And a topic's
past behaviour is not a promise about the next paper. This describes what UPSC
*has* asked, which is the best available guide, not what it *will*.
